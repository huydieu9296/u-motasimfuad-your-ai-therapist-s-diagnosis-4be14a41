A funny single-line diagnosis an AI therapist gives its human — deadpan, clinical, with the punchline landing at the end. Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-your-ai-therapist-s-diagnosis-4be14a41) (free entry, $2 prize pool paid down the top ten lines).

## Entry

> Diagnosis: acute main-character syndrome. You narrate your life like a documentary, but the camera crew quit in episode two and nobody told you.

144 characters, one line, text only.

## Why it works

- **A real diagnosis, in a therapist's voice:** it opens with "Diagnosis:" and a clinical label ("acute main-character syndrome"), so it reads like a chart note, not a stray joke.
- **The turn lands last:** the setup (narrating your life like a documentary) flips into the crew quitting in episode two — the laugh arrives at the end.
- **Fresh angle:** no "touch grass", no "have you tried turning yourself off and on again" — the documentary-crew image is a new take on main-character syndrome.
